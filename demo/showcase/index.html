About this demo

Every picture on Grain is drawn in the page as low-contrast dither: two close greys, never black on white, because fine high-contrast patterns can trigger pattern sensitivity too. The clips themselves are flat dark and mid grey, the same dim stimulus as the other FlashBan test pages.

FlashBan isn't told anything by this page. It finds the flashing by reading the pixels and the animations, counts flashes against the WCAG 2.3.1 limit by real time, and softens, covers or dims. Grain keeps no data and loads nothing from other sites.

FlashBan is not a medical device. It reduces exposure to flashing content; it can't guarantee protection.
