Get FlashBan

From every frame to a flat field.

FlashBan reads the pixels and animations already on the page, counts flashes by the clock, and usually acts in under a second.

The pipeline

  1. Sample every rendered frame

    Video frames, canvas pixels, animated images and CSS animations are read as they're drawn, never on a slow timer that a strobe could slip between.

  2. Measure brightness and red

    Relative luminance per WCAG, plus a separate check for saturated red, which can trigger seizures at lower brightness. Near-black noise is treated as having no colour.

  3. Count flashes in a one-second window

    By real time, not frame counts, so a slow machine can't make a strobe look slower than it is. Flashing area is added up across elements, so a big flash split into small tiles still counts.

  4. Decide

    Over the limit (WCAG by default; Strict and Relaxed are in the popup) means act. Can't read it at all means fail closed.

  5. Soften, cover or dim

    Soften keeps it playing with the flashing smoothed, checked again by a second detector. Cover pauses it behind a plain panel if softening isn't enough. Dim labels what couldn't be checked.

What you see on the page

  • Softened

    The default. A label says "Flashing softened"; the video, game or GIF keeps going.

  • Covered

    "Flashing content hidden" on a plain panel. To see the site as it is, press "Show original on this site" in FlashBan's bar. Pages can't press it for you: only real clicks count.

  • Can't verify

    Dimmed and labelled. Protected or cross-origin video can only be dimmed, not checked.

  • Locked down

    When a page keeps fighting the protection, its media is hidden and the toolbar icon shows "!".

Our own interface never flashes

No animations, no quick transitions, nothing that blinks. FlashBan's labels and panels respect reduced motion and forced colours, work by keyboard alone with a visible focus ring, and announce themselves to screen readers. Overlay text sits on solid chips: white on near-black (18:1) and dark on yellow (13.5:1).
