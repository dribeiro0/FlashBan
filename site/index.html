Get FlashBan

Flashing, reduced to an even grain.

FlashBan is a free Chrome extension for people with photosensitive epilepsy. It finds flashing video, GIFs, canvas and animation and softens just that part. The rest of the page stays sharp.

Download for Chrome Try it on the demo site

No account. No server. Not a medical device: FlashBan reduces exposure to flashing content, it can't guarantee protection.

What FlashBan does

  • Finds the flashing

    Video, canvas, animated images, CSS animation and script-driven flicker, measured against WCAG 2.3.1 (more than three flashes a second over a large area) and the separate red-flash rule, by real time.

  • Softens first

    Flashing video keeps playing through a smoothing filter, and a second detector checks the result. CSS animations slow to about one cycle a second. You keep watching.

  • Covers if it won't calm

    If softening can't get the flashing under the limit, FlashBan pauses it behind a plain panel. Only you can bring it back, with Show anyway.

  • Fails closed

    Media it can't read (protected or cross-origin video) is dimmed and labelled "can't verify", never silently let through.

  • Fights back

    A page that keeps deleting or undoing FlashBan's protection gets locked down: its media is hidden with styles the page can't see or remove.

Built to reveal as little as possible.

A seizure shield reveals a health condition, so FlashBan collects almost nothing. One honest gap: websites can still tell it is installed (see Known limits).

  • No account

    No sign-up, no identifiers, no keys.

  • No server

    Nothing about you is sent to us or anyone else.

  • A domain, 30 days

    The only record is a site's domain and a date, on your computer, deleted after 30 days. Clear it any time from the popup.
