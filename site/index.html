[ What it is ]

FlashBan is a free Chrome extension for people with photosensitive epilepsy. It finds flashing video, GIFs, canvas and animation and softens just that part, before it reaches you.

Soften first. Cover only if it won't calm down. Dim what it can't check. No account and no server: the only record is a site's domain, on your computer, for 30 days.

Not a medical device. It reduces exposure to flashing content; it can't guarantee protection.

From the changelog

Merged pull requests from the hackathon build, newest first. Every fix to a false alarm gets an everyday test page, so the harness keeps it fixed.

Nov 7

Near-black isn't red

Compression noise in dark scenes was being read as red flashing. Near-black now has no hue, so letterbox bars stop tripping the red-flash rule. (#94)

Nov 7

Sharper soften

The brightness clamp runs on the GPU at full resolution, only inside the flashing area. Everything outside it stays sharp and in colour. (#84)

Nov 7

YouTube, measured

YouTube video can now be read and measured instead of only dimmed, and ambient glows are faded rather than covered. (#77)

Nov 7

Every presented frame

Sampling every presented frame closed a gap where a 30 Hz strobe at 60 fps could slip between samples. (#65)

Nov 7

One bypass bar

A single "Show original on this site" bar replaces per-video buttons. Pages can't press it for you: only real clicks count. (#64)
