Get FlashBan

Built to be trusted, by collecting almost nothing.

Using a seizure shield reveals a health condition, and a browsing log shows where someone goes. So FlashBan's main protection is that it keeps almost nothing, and what it keeps never leaves your computer.

What FlashBan keeps

Everything is stored with Chrome's local extension storage, on your computer.

DataWhereHow long
Your settings (sensitivity, protection strength, per-site switch)this computeruntil you change them
Domains, dates, counts, peak flash estimates and source typesthis computer30 days, or until "Clear my data"
Accounts, identifiers, full URLs, page content, screenshotsnever collected–

The one network request

For a cross-origin .webp or .apng image the page is already loading, FlashBan fetches the file's first bytes without cookies to tell a still image from an animated one; only a yes or no comes back. It never reaches your own computer or local network: addresses like localhost, 192.168.x.x or 10.x.x.x are refused, and such images simply stay dimmed. GIFs are never probed: they always count as animated. There is no FlashBan server.

Known limits

Not a medical device. FlashBan reduces exposure to flashing content. It can't guarantee protection: detection takes a fraction of a second, and media it can't read can only be dimmed. Don't rely on it as your only protection.

  • Unreadable video

    DRM and cross-origin video without CORS can't be measured, only dimmed.

  • Detectable

    Websites can tell FlashBan is installed by probing its extension files, which can reveal a health condition. Fix (bundling the content scripts) not done yet.

  • First frames

    The first frames of a new flash can reach the screen before FlashBan reacts; response time depends on the page, source and machine.

  • Outside the browser

    Apps, games and video outside Chrome aren't covered.

  • Image probe

    The still-or-animated probe for WebP/APNG can be fooled by the image's server, and image hosts can see that this browser runs FlashBan.

  • Iframes

    Flashes split across iframes aren't added together; each frame is judged on its own.

  • Displays

    Brightness is computed from sRGB values. HDR, colour profiles and screen brightness aren't modelled.
